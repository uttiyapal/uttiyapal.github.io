# About Me

I am a researcher and geospatial analyst with a strong interest in remote sensing, GIS, and environmental monitoring. My academic and research journey has focused on understanding ecological dynamics through satellite imagery, spatial analysis, and geospatial modelling.

## Achievements

Some highlights of my academic and research journey include:

- Conducting research on advanced Landsat compositing techniques
- Working on ecosystem monitoring in the Sundarbans region
- Developing experience in GIS and remote sensing workflows
- Participating in academic and research-oriented projects

## Hobbies and Interests

Outside academics and research, I enjoy:

- Reading about geography, environment, and technology
- Exploring maps and geospatial visualizations
- Photography and nature observation
- Learning new computational and analytical tools
- Writing and maintaining academic and personal web projects
