# About Me

I am a researcher and geospatial analyst with a strong interest in remote sensing, GIS, and environmental monitoring. My academic and research journey has focused on understanding ecological dynamics through satellite imagery, spatial analysis, and geospatial modelling.

My work primarily explores coastal and mangrove ecosystems, with particular emphasis on the Indian Sundarbans. I am interested in combining Earth observation data with computational approaches to support environmental assessment and sustainable ecosystem management.

## Academic Background

My educational background is rooted in geography and geospatial sciences, where I developed expertise in:

- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Remote sensing and image processing
- Spatial statistics and environmental analysis
- Cartography and geovisualization
- Satellite-based ecological monitoring

Over time, my research interests evolved toward advanced applications of multispectral satellite data and long-term environmental monitoring.

## Research and Publications

My current research focuses on evaluating pixel-based compositing approaches such as:

- Best Available Pixel (BAP)
- Medoid compositing techniques

using Landsat imagery for mangrove ecosystem monitoring in the Indian Sundarbans, particularly Henry Island.
