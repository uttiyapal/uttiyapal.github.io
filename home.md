# Welcome

Hello! I am a researcher and geospatial analyst with interests in remote sensing, environmental monitoring, and spatial data science. My academic background and research work focus on applying geospatial technologies to understand ecological and environmental change.

## Education

I completed my undergraduate studies in geography and geospatial sciences, where I developed a strong foundation in cartography, GIS, and environmental analysis. I am currently pursuing advanced academic and research work focused on remote sensing applications, particularly in coastal and mangrove ecosystems.

## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Remote sensing and Earth observation
- Mangrove ecosystem monitoring
- Landsat and multispectral satellite data analysis
- Pixel-based compositing techniques
- Geospatial modelling and environmental change detection
- Coastal ecosystem dynamics in the Sundarbans

I am especially interested in integrating GIS, satellite imagery, and computational techniques for long-term environmental assessment and sustainable ecosystem management.

## Current Work

At present, I am working on a research project comparing Best Available Pixel (BAP) and Medoid compositing methods for mangrove monitoring using Landsat imagery in the Indian Sundarbans, with a focus on Henry Island.

Feel free to explore the rest of the site to learn more about my projects, publications, and research activities.
